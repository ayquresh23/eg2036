Phase changes with NaCl's transition temperatures

At the melting point (1074 K) added heat breaks the rigid lattice instead of stretching the bond: the energy goes into the enthalpy of fusion (orange bar) and the average spacing barely changes. Above the boiling point (1738 K) the bonds are overcome altogether. One generic well is scaled to NaCl's melting and boiling points, so treat this as an illustration, not a NaCl simulation.
