Why defects must exist

Each vacancy costs energy (the enthalpy line rises) but adds disorder (the −TΔS curve falls). The Gibbs energy is their sum. Its lowest point is the equilibrium number of defects.

Toy model in arbitrary energy units: ΔH = (cost) × (defect fraction), and −TΔS uses the ideal mixing entropy. In a real crystal the cost is about 1 eV per defect and kT at room temperature is 0.026 eV, so the minimum sits at roughly 1 site in 1017.
